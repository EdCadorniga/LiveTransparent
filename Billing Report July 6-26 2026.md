# LiveTransparent Work Report

## Billing Period

July 6, 2026 through July 26, 2026

## Summary

During this billing period, work focused on launching and stabilizing LiveTransparent's email, voice, SMS, LinkedIn, Instagram, and reporting systems. The work included campaign launches, customer-data cleanup, automation repairs, quality improvements, safety controls, monitoring, testing, and operational documentation.

The main business outcomes were:

- Email campaigns were launched and connected to contact tracking and reply suppression.
- The automated voice campaign was repaired, launched, and protected against duplicate or unwanted calls.
- Apollo phone enrichment was rebuilt so contacts could be researched and supplied with usable phone data again.
- LinkedIn and Instagram messaging was cleaned up, connected to the CRM inbox, and protected against duplicate or unwanted outreach.
- SimpleTexting SMS was launched through the CRM and improved for reliable delivery and reply handling.
- Executive reporting was expanded to include campaign, call, pipeline, social, and email activity.
- Obsolete or unsafe automation paths were disabled rather than left running.
