# Daily Quick Reference

Use this file during your work day.

## Pipeline Flow

`Warm` -> `Sales Outreach` -> `Sales`

## Your Main Goal

- Move the right leads forward
- Keep GHL clean
- Make sure no real lead gets stuck

## Daily Checklist

1. Review new leads in `Warm`
2. Check active leads in `Sales Outreach`
3. Push engaged leads toward a meeting
4. Move booked leads into the correct next stage
5. Close out stale leads the right way
6. Make sure every open lead has an owner and next step

## Warm Pipeline Quick Meanings

- `New`: not reviewed yet
- `Qualified (MQL)`: ready for sales attention
- `Routed to Outreach`: approved for active follow-up
- `Nurture Active`: still warm, but not ready
- `Disqualified`: not a fit right now

## Sales Outreach Quick Meanings

- `New`: just entered outreach
- `Attempting Contact`: follow-up is in progress
- `Engaged`: lead replied or interacted
- `Meeting Requested`: trying to lock in the meeting
- `Booked`: meeting is confirmed
- `Unresponsive`: no real reply after enough follow-up

## Sales Quick Meanings

- `Discovery Scheduled`: call is booked
- `Discovery Completed`: call happened
- `Proposal Sent`: proposal was sent
- `Negotiation`: terms are being discussed
- `Closed Won`: deal is in
- `Closed Lost`: deal is out

## MQL Quick Rules

- `mql` is not added to every warm lead
- Approved MQL paths are:
- LinkedIn Lead Form
- Meta Lead Form
- Website Hero/Footer forms
- Referral
- Booking only when calendar is `Regulated Ads On Social/Search`
- Normalized key may appear as `regulated-ads` or `regulated-ads-on-social-search`

## Booking Quick Rules

- Only `Regulated Ads On Social/Search` bookings should hit the `#leads` Slack alert
- The normalized key may appear as `regulated-ads` or `regulated-ads-on-social-search`
- Other bookings should not go to `#leads`
- Regulated ads bookings should also get tag `SQL`
- Regulated ads bookings should have an opportunity moved or created in `Sales -> Discovery Scheduled`
- Do not use `Warm  Referral` for regular bookings

## Validation Note

- This regulated ads booking flow was live-tested on `2026-03-19`
- The test contact and opportunity were left in GHL intentionally for team visibility

## Before You End Your Day

- No good lead should sit in the wrong stage
- No open lead should be missing an owner
- No active deal should be missing a next action
