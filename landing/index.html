FAQ

Questions brands ask us

Is advertising cannabis and kratom actually allowed on Meta, TikTok, and Google?

Yes — but it's tightly restricted and policy varies by platform, product, state, and claim. That's exactly the expertise we bring: we know what each platform accepts today and structure your ads so they pass review and stay up.

What makes this different from a regular ad agency?

Regulated industries fail for compliance reasons far more often than creative reasons. Standard agencies optimise for creative and spend; we optimise for compliant survival first, then performance — because a banned account converts nothing.

How fast can we launch?

Most brands are live with compliant campaigns within two weeks of the initial audit. If you already have history with the platforms, we can often move faster.

Do we own the ad accounts and pixels?

Yes. You own your business manager accounts, ad accounts, and pixels. We operate them under a partnership agreement — nothing is held hostage.

What do you need from us to start?

A 20-minute discovery call, access to your current ad accounts (or the go-ahead to build new ones), and your product/claims info. We handle the rest — including the compliance audit.
