# Email Open Follow-Up Process

This guide explains what happens when a contact opens emails many times but still does not book.

## Goal

If a contact opens emails 3 times:

- wait 45 minutes
- if they already booked, do nothing
- if they did not book, assign them to John

## Why This Matters

This helps the team notice warm leads who are paying attention but not taking the next step.

## Main Rule

Three opens do not always mean the lead is ready.

But three opens are strong enough to deserve a closer look.

## What the System Does

1. Counts email opens
2. Waits 45 minutes
3. Checks if the lead booked
4. If not booked:
- assigns the contact to John
- assigns or creates the opportunity for John

## Current Owner

- John

## Key Tags

- `Email Open 3x - Pending Assign`
- `open email 3x`

## Booked Guard

If the lead already booked:

- do nothing

This stops the team from working the same person like they are still unbooked.

## What Sales Should Know

- A lead with 3 opens may be interested
- Check the lead fast
- Do not double-work someone who already booked
- Look at notes, stage, and next action before making changes
