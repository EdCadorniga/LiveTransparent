# Sales Follow-Up Summary

## Purpose

Follow up with prospects who engaged with regulated-ads messaging or sales outreach. The sequence should educate, qualify interest, and direct a suitable prospect to the approved assessment with Cameron.

## Sequence

1. **Observation:** use a verified, relevant observation and ask whether the issue is active.
2. **Five-Point Checklist:** provide a useful diagnostic checklist without pretending to have audited the prospect.
3. **How We Diagnose:** explain the assessment process, compliance-account infrastructure, policy-aware creative and destinations, and monitoring.
4. **Permission Close:** close respectfully, invite correction of the contact, and honor opt-out requests.

## Operating Rules

- Use the approved sales sender identity and reply owner.
- Keep the CTA consistent with the direct Regulated Ads booking widget.
- Do not promise approval, ban prevention, performance, revenue, or timing.
- Stop outreach immediately on an opt-out, complaint, or suppression signal.
- Record replies, qualification, owner, source, next action, and due date in the CRM.
