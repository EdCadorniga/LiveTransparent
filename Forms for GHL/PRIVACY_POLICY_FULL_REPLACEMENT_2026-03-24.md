# Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 24, 2026

Transparent eCom LLC respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide through this website, our forms, our booking tools, and our communications channels.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we share it, how long we retain it, and what choices you may have regarding your information.

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

- Contact information, such as your name, email address, phone number, company name, and website
- Message and inquiry information, such as the information you submit in website forms, demo requests, booking requests, or contact forms
- Technical information, such as IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, page URL, referral source, and approximate geolocation
- Usage and analytics information, such as page visits, clicks, session activity, campaign attribution data, and form interaction events
- Communications and consent records, such as marketing opt-ins, non-marketing operational consent, unsubscribes, timestamps, and delivery or routing logs

## 2. How We Collect Information

We may collect information:

- directly from you when you complete a form, request a demo, book a meeting, contact us, or otherwise submit information through the site
- automatically through cookies, pixels, scripts, server-side tracking, analytics tools, and similar technologies
- from service providers and platforms that support our CRM, scheduling, communications, analytics, and advertising infrastructure

## 3. How We Use Information

We may use your information to:

- respond to inquiries and demo requests
- contact you about scheduling and operational follow-up
- provide, manage, and improve our services
- personalize website and marketing experiences
- measure campaign performance and website conversions
- maintain CRM records and internal reporting
- send marketing emails or marketing text messages where you have opted in or where otherwise permitted by law
- detect abuse, maintain security, and comply with legal or regulatory obligations

## 4. Website Forms, Booking Requests, and Communications

When you submit a website form, request a demo, or interact with our booking widgets, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, company name, website, message content, and related submission metadata.

We use this information to respond to your request, route the inquiry internally, schedule meetings, send operational communications related to your request, and maintain records of communications and consent.

If you choose to opt in to marketing communications, we may use your contact information to send promotional emails and text messages about our services, updates, and offers. Consent to receive marketing text messages is not required to submit a request, schedule a meeting, or purchase services.

We may maintain records of:

- form submissions
- booking requests
- consent selections
- opt-in and opt-out activity
- timestamps
- message delivery activity
- routing and processing logs

## 5. Cookies, Pixels, Tracking, and Advertising Technologies

We may use cookies, pixels, tags, scripts, server-side tracking, conversion APIs, analytics tools, and similar technologies to:

- understand website usage
- measure ad and campaign performance
- improve conversion tracking
- optimize audience targeting
- build or refine advertising audiences where permitted by law

These technologies may associate your website activity or form submissions with campaigns, channels, ads, or referral sources.

You may be able to limit certain tracking through browser settings, privacy settings, cookie controls, or device-level preferences, though some site features may be affected.

## 6. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information in the ordinary sense of selling customer lists for cash. We may, however, share information with vendors, platforms, and service providers that help us operate our business and website, including providers that support:

- website hosting
- CRM and automation
- scheduling and booking
- analytics and attribution
- advertising and audience measurement
- email delivery
- SMS delivery
- customer support
- internal business operations

These providers may process information on our behalf or under their own roles depending on the service involved.

## 7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for:

- business operations
- communications
- recordkeeping
- legal compliance
- dispute resolution
- security
- analytics and reporting

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information, the context in which it was collected, and applicable legal obligations.

## 8. SMS and Email Communications

If you provide your contact information through our site, we may send you operational messages related to your request, inquiry, scheduling, or services.

If you opt in to marketing communications:

- you may receive marketing emails from us
- you may receive marketing text messages from us

You may opt out of:

- marketing emails by using the unsubscribe link included in the email
- marketing text messages by replying STOP

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply depending on your carrier and plan.
