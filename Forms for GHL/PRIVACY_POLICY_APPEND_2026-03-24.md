## Website Forms, Demo Requests, and Communications

When you submit a form, request a demo, or interact with our booking widgets on this website, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, company name, website, message content, and related metadata such as page URL, timestamp, device/browser data, and consent selections.

We use this information to respond to inquiries, schedule demos, provide requested services, send operational communications related to your request, improve our website experience, and measure marketing performance.

If you choose to opt in to marketing communications, we may use your contact information to send promotional emails and text messages about our services, updates, resources, and related offers. Consent to receive marketing text messages is not required to submit a request, book a demo, or purchase services from us.

We may maintain records of form submissions, booking requests, consent selections, opt-in events, opt-out events, timestamps, and related routing or delivery logs for compliance, support, and operational purposes.

We may share this information with service providers and platforms that help us operate our website, forms, scheduling, customer relationship management, analytics, advertising, and communications infrastructure. These providers may include website hosting vendors, CRM and automation platforms, scheduling providers, email service providers, SMS delivery providers, analytics tools, and advertising platforms.

If you no longer wish to receive marketing emails from us, you may use the unsubscribe link included in those emails. If you no longer wish to receive marketing text messages from us, you may reply STOP to opt out. For help with text messaging, reply HELP where supported or contact us directly.

We retain personal data collected through website forms and communications only for as long as reasonably necessary for business, legal, operational, and compliance purposes.

For privacy-related questions or requests regarding information submitted through this website, contact us at `cameron@livetransparent.com` unless and until we publish a different privacy contact address.

## Tracking, Attribution, and Advertising

We may use cookies, pixels, server-side tracking, Conversion APIs, and related attribution tools to understand website usage, measure campaign performance, improve conversion reporting, and build or optimize advertising audiences where permitted by law.

These technologies may associate website form submissions or website visits with campaigns, channels, or referral sources for analytics and marketing performance reporting.
